More Than a Studio. A Community.

Serenity Flow was born from a simple belief: yoga should feel like coming home. Here's our story.

How Serenity Flow Began

In 2018, Elena Marsh was a burned-out marketing director who walked into a yoga class on a whim and left an hour later feeling like a different person. She spent the next two years studying, training, and teaching at studios across the country before opening Serenity Flow in Springfield in 2020.

What she wanted to create was the opposite of the intimidating, performance-focused studios she'd encountered early on. A place where a 60-year-old beginner and a seasoned practitioner could share the same mat space without anyone feeling out of place.

Six years later, Serenity Flow has become the heart of Springfield's wellness community — with over 400 active members, three expert instructors, and a waiting list for weekend Yin classes.

Our Mission
  • Make yoga genuinely accessible — in price, atmosphere, and instruction style — for every body and background.
  • Build a community that supports each other on and off the mat, not just a place to exercise.
  • Prioritize the long-term wellbeing of every student over class counts, advanced poses, or aesthetics.
  • Create a studio space that feels like a sanctuary — one that people leave feeling better than when they arrived, every single time.

What We Stand For

The values that shape every class, every decision, and every conversation at Serenity Flow.

Radical Inclusivity

No prerequisites. No perfect bodies. No judgment. Whether you're 25 or 75, flexible or stiff as a board, this is your space.

Growth at Your Own Pace

We never push students beyond their readiness. Progress in yoga is deeply personal — our job is to create the conditions for it, not to rush it.

Community First

A great studio isn't just about classes. It's about the people who share those classes. We invest in events, connections, and a culture that lasts beyond the mat.
