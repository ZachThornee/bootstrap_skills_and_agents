Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first class.

Absolutely. Most of our classes are designed for all levels and our instructors offer modifications for every pose. We also run dedicated Beginner's Yoga workshops each month that walk you through the fundamentals at a comfortable pace. Your first class is free — come as you are.

Comfortable, stretchy clothing and a water bottle. Mat rental is included with every membership and drop-in — we provide Manduka mats at no extra charge. You're welcome to bring your own mat if you prefer. Lockers and changing rooms are available on site.

We offer both heated and non-heated rooms. Hot yoga classes are held in our Ember Studio, kept at 95–100°F. All other classes are held in our main Flow Studio at room temperature. The class listing will always indicate which room is used so you can choose what's right for you.

Classes can be booked online through our scheduling system or via our mobile app. We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance as popular sessions fill quickly. Drop-ins are welcome when space allows, but booking ahead guarantees your spot and your mat.

Monthly memberships can be paused for up to 60 days per year (for travel, injury, or life) and cancelled with 30 days' notice — no fees, no hassle. Annual memberships can be paused for injury with a doctor's note. We believe your practice should feel like a choice, not a contract.
