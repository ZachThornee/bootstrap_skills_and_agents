Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents and students want to know before booking.

We tutor all math subjects from grades 4 through 12, including elementary arithmetic, pre-algebra, algebra 1 & 2, geometry, pre-calculus, calculus, statistics, and AP courses. We also offer dedicated SAT and ACT math prep.

Standard sessions are 60 minutes. For younger students (grades 4–6) we recommend starting with 45-minute sessions to maintain focus. Intensive exam prep sessions can be extended to 90 minutes on request.

Most students notice improved confidence and understanding within 2–3 sessions. Grade improvements typically show within 4–6 weeks of regular sessions. Students using the Accelerate or Intensive packages see results faster due to session frequency.
